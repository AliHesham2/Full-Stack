# Project role entry

Read [Roster](workspace/rostar/roster.md) before acting. It is the canonical record of the owner's approved role assignments and links each role's required starting prompt.

Follow your explicitly approved assignment to its `prompt.md`, then its `skillz/SKILL.md` and required task-relevant sources. A blank assignment is unassigned: ask for the genuinely missing role/operator decision rather than appointing yourself or another agent. A client filename, model name or chat provider does not assign a role.

This file is an entry pointer only. Responsibilities, procedures and delivery rules belong to the linked role packages, not a duplicated workflow here. Reading this entry or Roster grants no tools, communication, dispatch, live side effects or release authority beyond actual owner/host permissions.
